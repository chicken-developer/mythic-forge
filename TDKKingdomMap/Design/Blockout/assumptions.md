# Thành A — assumptions v1

- Actual installed engine: UE **5.8.3**, CL 58210709. Original project is Blank Blueprint with empty Content; Engine GameModeBase/DefaultPawn was the fallback (flying sphere, not walking). A native first-person Character is supplied for this blockout: capsule radius 0.42 m / half height 0.96 m, step 0.35 m, walkable angle 35°, speed 6 m/s. No AI requirement was found, so NavMesh is not mandatory for this revision.
- The four existing files have `.png.png` suffixes. No renaming is needed. Reference 01 is primary. Reference 02 informs subordinate massing; 03/04 inform roofs, elongated Gothic volumes and towers. HDA labels in the references do not imply a Houdini dependency.
- Water datum **0 m is an assumption**. Seabed is -6 m. Blue architecture polygons are Medieval land, including the lower gate district and waterfront; they are not water masks.
- The ruler in reference 01 is estimated as 200 m across 240 pixels (x=25..265, y=1017). Initial plan scale is 0.833333 m/pixel, independently adjustable in layout.json. The illustration is not a survey; wall centreline, contour, road, quay and footprint coordinates are approximate. No coordinates are taken from the perspective inset.
- Image crop is [105, 0, 1033, 1000] in the original 1448×1086 image (right/bottom exclusive). Plaza origin is [580,450] pixels. Image-to-plan matrix is stored in layout.json; screen Y is inverted to +Y North.
- City walls follow the traced main image, approximately 660 m East–West and 596 m North–South. Gothic density and building size are increased **inside** the traced red polygon; the red area is not expanded. Green is a consistent intermediate kit, with every fifth grid position eligible for paired towers.
- Representative flat pads are selected within the requested ground ranges: gate district 2 m, market 6 m (main route rises to 8 m), residential/craft/plaza 8 m, forge 12 m, upper tier/council/barracks 18 m, watchtower 22 m, waterfront 2 m, village 5 m, mill 7 m, quarry 9 m. Flat district pads are a blockout simplification. Road profiles override ground only within their own corridors. Heights are not inferred from architecture colors.
- Roads 6–18 m wide (watchtower branch 6 m, port 8 m), main gate 18×8 m clear opening, posterns 8–10×5–6 m, walls 3×10 m, towers radius 8 m / wall-body height 17 m. Lower port routing follows an estimated switchback corridor; dimensions and exact bends require design review. The 20 m waterfront passage is the interpreted open pedestrian connection at the illustrated waterfront descent, not a measured gate. All values are data controls.
- Stair rise 0.25 m, width 5 m, landings 4 m. Ramp end landings are width/2 + 0.8 m in the initial data (3.8–9.8 m), with explicit editable landing_m on every route. Design ramp ceiling 12° provides margin below Character 35°. The compiler reports the required horizontal run from rise / tan(12°) after subtracting the landings. A route with insufficient run is rejected. Current maximum slope with landings is 11.132°. Flat round junction pads keep corners continuous.
- House dimensions, spacing, roof heights, tower ratios, jitter ≤2 m, clearance 2.5 m and seed 17429 are estimated blockout kit parameters. Box bodies are closed; no interior, windows or finished material/detail work. A house is rejected if its foundation corners differ by over 0.25 m in ground elevation.
- Landscape grid is 1009×1009 at 1 m spacing, 16×16 components with one 63-quad section. The encoded interval is [-32,32) m; uint16 32768 = Z0. Actor offset (-50400,-50400,0) cm, scale (100,100,12.5), rotation zero. South is raw/PNG row zero; do not flip Y. Within paved/stair corridors, `roadbed_depth_m=0.25` lowers the Landscape substrate beneath the 0.4 m mesh slabs to prevent heightfield interpolation clipping their tops. Walking surfaces are the design profile +0.04 m; roadbed samples are reported separately from that walking datum. District/building pads retain their stated ground elevations. Explicit road/stair meshes and retaining walls keep collision and vertical boundaries legible; no noise or grayscale concept heightmap is used.
- Harbor piers are estimated 8–10 m wide and extend over sea. Sea is an opaque debug slab below Z0, without collision; it is not a Water System simulation.
- External hamlet polygons and elevations are recorded, but houses are confined within the city boundary as requested. Outside terrain is provisional; no claim of a surveyed coastline is made.

Implementation constants (kit shape ratios, solid road thickness 0.4 m, foundation embed 0.5 m) are reusable kit construction choices rather than traced measurements. They are documented here to distinguish them from ground heights.

Lighting is a provisional daytime setup: movable atmospheric sun at 50,000 lux, rotation (-48,-35,0), Sky Atmosphere, light exponential height fog (density 0.005), realtime SkyLight intensity 1.2 and shadowless opposite fill at 15,000 lux. Parameters and capture exposure bias -12 are editable in `layout.json.lighting`. This fill improves readability of primitive facades; it is not final lighting art. Landscape supplies the island ground and coastline; the former thin perimeter box strips have been removed. Remaining Terrain mesh actors are retaining walls and the noncolliding water datum slab, not a terrain proxy.
